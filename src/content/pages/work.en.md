## How these case studies are written

Each case study is written from the scope on record: the modules built, the integrations connected, the role I held and the status of the system today. If a figure is not on record, it is not on the page: no percentages, no user counts, no outcomes I cannot show you.

In place of screenshots, each page carries a system schematic: custom modules, the standard apps they touch and the direction of the data between them, drawn so a technical reader sees what was configured and what was built.

Attribution is stated on every page: the hotel system was delivered while I was at IRAM in Latakia and runs at Grand Hotel Tartus, named because it is on my CV; the other two were delivered as an employee of iLines Solutions in Riyadh, their end clients described by sector only.

## Hotel management system on Odoo

Hospitality · In production · 2024

Custom Odoo modules forming a property-management system for Grand Hotel Tartus. Reservations check room availability and open a guest folio; rate plans move with demand and occupancy; point of sale runs across the hotel's outlets with charges accumulating on the folio; and an automated night audit posts the day's revenue and closes the operating day. Staff were trained on the delivered system.

[Read the hotel case study](/work/hotel-management-system/)

## Portal POS with Tap payments

Portal and payments · Client deployment · 2025

A lightweight point of sale inside the Odoo portal. Users browse a catalogue, place simplified orders and follow their status without back-office access. Payment runs through the Tap gateway by redirect and callback; the transaction state is reconciled and the posting into Sales and Accounting is automatic. Built as an employee of iLines Solutions on controllers, QWeb templates, JavaScript and a custom order model bridging into Sales.

[Read the portal POS case study](/work/portal-pos-tap-payments/)

## Construction project suite on Odoo 19

Contracting · In progress · 2025 to present

For contracting clients on Odoo 19: project modules for cost codes on the CSI structure, BOQ comparison, manpower analytics and management dashboards, plus one validation mixin, written once and inherited by the purchase request, the receipt and the payment document, which refuses any of them once the spending limit is reached. The modules run on live projects, so each module upgrade ships with scripts that move the project data with it.

[Read the construction case study](/work/construction-project-suite/)

## Which case study to read first

Moving onto Odoo: the hotel, where most of the front office had to be built. Standard Odoo almost fits but needs a gateway or device connected: the portal POS. Custom modules that must survive upgrades: the construction suite.

## Three sectors, one method

The method behind all three systems was the same: map the operation with its stakeholders, keep standard Odoo wherever it fits, build only the remainder as inheritance-based modules. Phases and terms are on [How I work](/how-i-work/), the practice areas on [Services](/services/).

Odoo work reaches me in four shapes: a company moving onto Odoo, a company whose standard Odoo almost fits, a company held on an old version, and a company that is live and wants a named person behind the system. I cover all four myself, as an Odoo developer and ERP functional consultant employed at iLines Solutions in Riyadh (remote) and available for freelance projects. Working techno-functionally means the person who wrote your specification is the person who builds it, so the gap between the functional and technical sides never opens.

## Implementation: you are moving onto Odoo

Choose this when finance, HR and payroll, sales, purchasing, inventory, projects or POS are going onto Odoo for the first time, or when an earlier rollout stalled between the requirements and the go-live. The work runs from the requirements workshop through configuration, data migration, UAT and go-live, with the apps set up around your processes. For Saudi companies it includes Saudi localisation and ZATCA e-invoicing through Odoo's standard modules. Read about [Odoo implementation](/services/odoo-implementation/).

## Customisation: standard Odoo almost fits

Choose this when the system is in place but a process does not match the standard app, a report does not exist, an approval chain needs enforcing, or Odoo has to exchange data with a payment gateway, an attendance device or an external system. Custom work is inheritance-based and never edits core, so it survives the next upgrade. Integrations sit inside this engagement. Read about [Odoo customisation and integration](/services/odoo-customization/).

## Migration: the version is holding you back

Choose this when you are on Odoo 14 to 17 and need 18 or 19, when custom modules no longer load on the target version, or when live data has to be carried across a module upgrade. You keep working on the old version while the upgrade runs on a copy, and your key users sign off the copy before the switch. Read about [Odoo migration](/services/odoo-migration/).

## Support: the system is live and needs a named person

Choose this after go-live, when the first payroll run, the first invoice batch and the first stock count need someone watching them with your users, and when you want the documentation and repository to outlive the engagement. A retainer can be agreed once the system settles. Read about [Odoo support after go-live](/services/odoo-support/).

## What every engagement includes

Every engagement begins with discovery and a written scope, is built and demonstrated on a staging database, and ends with documentation and a repository you own. The six phases and the terms of engagement are set out in full on [how I work](/how-i-work/).

## If you are not sure which engagement fits

A short description of your company and the processes involved is enough for me to say which engagement applies, or whether Odoo is the wrong tool, before any scope is written. Editions, versions and pricing are covered on the [FAQ](/faq/); who owns the code and the data is answered on the [home page](/).

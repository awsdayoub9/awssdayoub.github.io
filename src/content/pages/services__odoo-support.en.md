## What if the freelancer disappears

Every company weighs this before hiring one person instead of a firm, and it deserves a direct answer rather than a reassurance.

- **A named person.** You have my number, and I reply in Arabic or English during UTC+3 working hours. I work remotely for iLines Solutions in Riyadh and take freelance projects alongside that work; both facts are stated so you can judge my availability.
- **A handover pack.** Every engagement ends with documentation, admin access and the repository in your name; the contents are listed below.
- **Training.** Your key users and your administrator are trained on the delivered system, in Arabic or English, before handover.
- **A retainer, if you want one.** A support retainer can be agreed after go-live. The engagement models are described on [how I work](/how-i-work/).
- **Availability.** Available for new projects and for support of systems already live.

## Hypercare in the first weeks after go-live

Hypercare is the period straight after cut-over, when the system meets real transactions:

- Issues raised by HR, finance and procurement users are logged, reproduced on staging and fixed or configured.
- Fixes are released to production in batches you approve, not one hot patch at a time.
- The first payroll run, the first invoice batch and the first stock count are watched with the user who owns them.

Hypercare ends by agreement, once the recurring issues have stopped and your users are working without me on the line.

## UAT cycles and issue resolution

UAT is not a single sign-off. Each cycle runs your key users through the specification on the test database, collects what fails and returns a fixed build for the next cycle. After go-live the same loop continues more slowly, and the classification of each issue is written down, because that is where support goes wrong.

## How a support request is triaged

Every request lands in one of three lanes. Naming the lane first keeps hypercare from turning into an unpaid rebuild, and keeps configuration questions from being billed as code.

| Lane | What it looks like | What happens | Who decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fix | Delivered scope does not behave as specified: a report totals wrongly, a workflow skips an approver | Reproduced on staging, corrected, released in the next batch; covered by hypercare or the retainer | Me, against the specification |
| Configuration question | Odoo can already do it, but nobody knew where: a new tax, a new user, a new approval threshold | Answered; where it recurs, added to the documentation and shown to your administrator | Me, with your administrator |
| Change request | A new report, rule or integration, or a change to how the process works | Written up with scope and effect on existing modules; approved by you before work starts | You |

## What the handover pack contains

The handover pack is the answer to the first objection on this page. It is delivered at go-live and updated at the end of hypercare, and typically contains:

- The specification as built, each requirement marked configured or custom, with deviations from the original scope recorded.
- Configuration notes per app: settings changed from default and why, taxes, salary rules, approval thresholds.
- The custom modules in a repository you own, each with its README and its migration scripts.
- A data-migration record: what was loaded, from where, and the validation signed off.
- Admin access and the access-rights matrix: which role sees what, and how to add a user without breaking it.
- User guides for the processes your staff run daily, in Arabic or English, written from the UAT scripts.
- The integration data flows, with test credentials, callback addresses and what to check when one fails.
- Training material and a record of who was trained on what.
- An open-items list: known limitations, deferred requests and change requests awaiting approval.

## Working hours and channels

I keep UTC+3 working hours and reply in Arabic or English. WhatsApp is the quickest channel for anything urgent during hypercare; email is for anything that should be on record, such as a change request or a sign-off. Every issue is logged in one list you can see, whichever channel it arrived through.

## After hypercare

When hypercare ends you choose: carry on with your administrator and the handover pack, or agree a retainer so a named person stays on call. The models, without prices, are on [how I work](/how-i-work/).

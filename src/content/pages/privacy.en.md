## What is not collected

This site sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. The fonts are served from the site itself, so no request goes to a font service when a page loads.

## GitHub Pages logs

The pages are static files served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, including visitor IP addresses, for security and legal reasons under its own privacy statement. I have no access to those logs and receive no report from them.

## What stays in your browser

Two preferences are saved in your browser's local storage: the theme you choose, light or dark, and the language you last read the site in. They never leave your device, they are not sent to me or to anyone else, and clearing your browser's site data removes them.

## The contact form

The form on the home page asks for your name, your email address, what you need and your message; the company field is optional. When you press send, the submission goes to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service, which emails it to awsdayoub1@gmail.com. FormSubmit processes the submission under its own privacy policy, and I do not control what it retains. A hidden field in the form catches automated submissions and holds nothing for a human visitor.

Once the message reaches my mailbox, I use it for one purpose: to reply to you about the work you asked about. It stays in my mailbox while our conversation is relevant, and you can ask me to delete it at any time.

## WhatsApp, email and external links

The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with a message already typed, addressed to +963 983 354 124. Nothing is sent until you press send inside WhatsApp, and from that point the conversation is governed by WhatsApp's terms and privacy policy. Email sent to awsdayoub1@gmail.com is handled by Google under its terms. Links to LinkedIn and GitHub take you to those services, each with its own policy.

## Correction and deletion

To see what I hold about you, correct it or have it deleted, email awsdayoub1@gmail.com from the address you used to contact me. I will confirm by reply when it is done. I do not sell your details, share them with third parties beyond the services named above, or use them for marketing.

This notice is written with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law and the EU General Data Protection Regulation in mind. It is a plain description of what this site does, not legal advice.

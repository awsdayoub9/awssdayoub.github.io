## What I do

Since 2024 I have taken ERP work from requirement to production, on both sides of the project. On the functional side I sit with stakeholders and consultants, map their processes to standard Odoo and document the gaps in a specification. On the technical side I build what the gaps need: Python models on the ORM, XML views, QWeb PDF and Excel reports, OWL and JavaScript components, HTTP and JSON controllers and REST integrations. The areas I have worked in are HR and payroll, accounting, sales, purchasing, inventory, projects and point of sale, with experience within a large multi-company Odoo suite.

My integration experience covers payment gateways, biometric attendance devices and government platforms; the delivered example is Tap, connected to Odoo with automatic reconciliation into Sales and Accounting. On versions, I have migrated databases and custom modules from Odoo 14 to 18 and to 19, and current build work is on 19.

## Why one person for the functional and the technical side

The market's term for this is techno-functional: the same person runs the workshop, writes the functional specification and then builds what it describes, so nothing is lost between the two sides.

Many Odoo projects split the work between a consultant who gathers the requirements and a developer who builds them, and the specification is where detail is lost between the two. Doing both sides myself means the person who heard the requirement is the person who maps it to standard Odoo, writes up the gap and builds only what the gap needs; nothing is translated twice.
